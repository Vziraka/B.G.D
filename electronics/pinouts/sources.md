# Electrical source and symbol register — 2026-09-05

Scope: selected Q75/R1.1 modules and their documented electrical terminals. **Module symbols are functional schematics, not footprint or mating-face drawings.** Numeric pins are used only for TI PDIP14, the Pi physical J8 header and two-terminal passives. Manufacturer-named terminals are retained for modules. Unknown connector cavity numbers, lead exits and physical orientations are not assigned. No symbol authorizes a PCB or replaces a received-part check.
