## Supply tree and established decisions

One factory6S2P pack feeds the separate genuine single-pack QS8 male. The positive path crosses INA-BM before every aircraft load. Common negative bypasses the shunt. BUS_POS feeds the AIO and two separately fused external regulators. **AIO_VBAT is an endpoint on BUS_POS, not another regulator output.** The aliases in the old sheet plan resolve as COMPUTE_5V2=PI_5V2, PERIPHERAL_5V=PER_5V, PI_3V3_LOGIC=PI_3V3 and PI_FAN5V2=PI_5V2. SOLENOID_POS is the same equipotential rail as PER_5V through DRV1 LOAD HIGH; SOLENOID_LOW remains isolated from GND by the switched MOSFET.

The schematic establishes nominal independent positive rails and the documented UART/PWM/I2C connections. Ground labels represent connectivity; the harness's dedicated return conductors remain compulsory. Collapsing GND in a schematic does not prove return-wire sizing or fault tolerance. No new SPI/CAN bus, receiver path, connector family or PCB replaces the baseline.

## Protection, voltage and signal calculations

- 6S full-charge arithmetic is 25.2 V at 4.2 V/cell. The selected 32 V fuses and 35 V main capacitor clear that nominal DC value; **this is not transient or fault-interruption qualification**. Main power remains unfused under Q75/R1.1. QS8 anti-spark is neither reverse-polarity protection nor a fuse.
- INA's200µΩ typical shunt dissipates approximately1.477W at the existing85.9232A modeled upper corner, and2.307W at the107.404A component-rating screen. Joint resistance and installation cooling are additional. The150A component rating is not permission to operate the aircraft there.
- U1's documented2V minimum HIGH/0.8V maximum LOW supports nominal3.3V-to5V translation. Actual input/output edges, grounding and independent-rail sequence still need validation. Grounding OEs and input pulldowns prevents floating commands; it does not establish crash-safe lamps or release.
- Nominal closed-switch GPIO voltage is `3.3*330/(4700+330)=0.2165V`. Nominal rising RC time constant is470µs; closing time constant is `(4700||330)*100nF=30.84µs`. This is a circuit calculation, not a selected debounce interval, transient-immunity proof or detection of ground shorts.
- F1/F2 are5A **input** fuses. They do not protect every thinner output branch by themselves: a buck converter can deliver greater output current than input current. Time-current curves, fault current, clearing energy, regulator current-limit dynamics, wire insulation/temperature and connector limits must be reconciled before release. Do not move a fuse to the return or equate an ERC pass with protection coordination.
- At the illustrative18V bus and typical88% efficiency, full5A compute output draws approximately1.641A plus quiescent; at25.2V approximately1.172A. These are efficiency scenarios only and cannot prove nuisance-trip margin, inrush performance or safety under faults.
