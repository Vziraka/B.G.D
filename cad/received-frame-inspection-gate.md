## Release rule

Every applicable row needs measured evidence, a reconciled model and an explicit pass, or a separately justified non-sensitive status. Unfinished design is not a measurement pass. If the received topology differs, redesign only the affected interface while preserving Q75/Q74. Bench, vibration, insulation, thermal and flight acceptance remain separate gates. Explicit procurement/fabrication/physical-operation authorization is still required when applicable.

## Tolerant detail policy

Use replaceable B.G.D. adapters and a measured-hole drilling variant. Reserve captured, matched nonconductive shims at custom support stations; do not add loose shim piles or distort the factory frame. Slotting is permitted only after bearing area, edge ligament, alignment and clamp/slip loads are checked; this review does not release enlarged/slotted holes or approve reduced thread engagement. Preserve service loops without transferring load to pads or allowing wire intrusion into props. Keep the future battery/CG adjustment independent of unmeasured frame hole locations.
