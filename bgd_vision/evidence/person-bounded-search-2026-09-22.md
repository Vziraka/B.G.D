# Person recovery: bounded search, 22 September 2026

Owner decision Q6 authorizes automatic recovery after longer disappearance when consistent evidence supports the same selected person, with ambiguity retained as uncertainty. This changes the former requirement for every clear exit to latch Lost immediately. It does not qualify aircraft movement or biometric identity.

`TargetSelection(..., recovery_enabled=True, search_window=10.0)` opts into the new policy. Omitting `search_window` preserves the previous three-second policy. The ten-second engineering limit matches the existing object's bounded search duration and provides time for a brief desk exit/return; it is not a measured identity-validity guarantee.

- A clear disappearance starts Searching. Brief crossings retain their existing Temporarily hidden behavior for three seconds, then become Searching without resetting the absolute deadline.
- Camera/inference freshness remains 750 ms. Stale observations or the absolute deadline latch Lost, erase the appearance references, and require manual selection. Clear/reselection cancels the previous search.
- Searching is not Tracking and supplies no trusted target claim. The original descriptor is frozen. Existing proximity, plausible height, overlap, current-candidate margin and remembered-competitor margins remain required.
- Searching requires at least five distinct exposures over 350 ms within a 500 ms confirmation interval, all with cosine similarity at least 0.95. A different ID, inconsistent candidate position, weak crop or failed gate restarts confirmation. The short-occlusion path retains its original 0.90/three-observation/150 ms rule.
- A previously solo person may return alone after an empty scene. Seeing multiple people during search permanently removes this exception. After a crossing, known competitors must be separately visible, and their frozen appearance evidence must still distinguish the target. Missing comparison evidence stays uncertain until timeout or explicit reselection.

Validation: 57 selector tests passed, including all 46 previous regressions. New tests cover a four-second solo disappearance, prolonged crossing, ambiguity, missing competitors, remembered lookalikes, weak/invalid appearance, implausible location/size, absolute timeout, stale capture, duplicate exposure timestamps, jumping same-ID candidates, Clear and parameter validation. The full vision suite passed 126 tests with four platform skips before the final isolated jumping-candidate regression was added; the focused 57-test run includes that last regression.

No new models, dependencies, background worker or extra camera inference were added. No Pi activity occurred for this change. Available retained public-video reports lack the original per-frame descriptors needed to replay this policy, and no local video/model fixtures were found under `tmp/` or `bgd_vision/`; those historical reports are not evidence for this revision.

Physical qualification remains open: similarity thresholds are conservative engineering choices, not calibrated false-match rates. Similar clothing, unknown lookalikes, changed pose/light and camera movement can still confuse or reject appearance matching. The proximity gate deliberately prevents automatic recovery at arbitrary distant positions. One batched desk test should cover return after 4–8 seconds, wrong-person entry, two similar candidates, a >10-second return that remains Lost, Stop during search, and stopped capture; never treat this as flight readiness. In every uncertain case check that no green selected-target box appears.
