#### Soldering practice session — preparation, not permission to energize aircraft

Plan roughly **60–90 minutes for a first learning session**, not a qualification guarantee. Use a ventilated heat-resistant surface, iron stand and eye protection; keep battery and flight electronics disconnected and away. Use spare/offcut wire only after reserving harness lengths, a cheap solder-practice board/perfboard with pads, and a spare connector that is not needed for the aircraft. Add a small practice board to the shopping list; it is not already owned. We guide one exercise at a time once equipment is present.

1. Prepare/tin two spare wire ends: strip without cutting strands, apply electronics flux and coat copper with solder. Learn to heat the work so solder wets the copper instead of forming a loose blob.
2. Make a wire-to-wire joint, let it cool undisturbed, inspect all sides, perform a gentle mechanical pull and measure continuity. Fit heatshrink only after review; ensure no sharp strand or exposed conductor remains.
3. Solder a short wire/component lead to a practice-board pad; check for bridges and pad damage. Remove excess solder with wick and redo one joint to practice correction.
4. With the broad tip, practice the actual thick-wire class on a spare disconnected connector. Pass only with solder wetting both surfaces, stable mechanical retention and no melted connector/insulation; continuous heating to force an unsuccessful joint is not a pass.
5. Proposed learning exit: three successive acceptable examples of each joint type needed next. This is a teaching checkpoint, not flight certification. Visual/tug/continuity checks cannot establish high-current heating performance; final harness still needs staged loaded electrical checks. If repeats fail, continue on scrap or seek hands-on help before touching the flight controller.

Sources for technique and flight sequencing: [Adafruit joint technique](https://learn.adafruit.com/adafruit-guide-excellent-soldering/making-a-good-solder-joint), [common joint defects](https://learn.adafruit.com/adafruit-guide-excellent-soldering/common-problems), [ArduPilot pre-arm checks](https://ardupilot.org/copter/docs/common-prearm-safety-checks.html), [initial flight](https://ardupilot.org/copter/docs/initial-tuning-flight.html). Local planning/learning criteria above are engineering proposals, not quoted certification standards.
