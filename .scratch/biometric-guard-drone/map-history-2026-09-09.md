Q75 explicitly permits hardware-interface, fabrication and safety artifacts spanning installed later-feature hardware; it does not authorize software implementation or physical operation. The later roadmap still includes requested voice flight, onboard vision, follow-me, selected-subject commands and area patrol, handheld, guest access, and authorization-dependent aspirations; later milestones get their own detailed efforts.
- Cloud/internet-dependent operation — the offline constraint is foundational.
