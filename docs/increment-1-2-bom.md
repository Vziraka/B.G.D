### Mass and flight time

Verified listed frame/motor/propeller masses plus a conservative nominal battery mass total **1,099 grams before electronics and mounts**: 173 + 4×54.3 + 4×9.7 + 670. The battery listing has conflicting mass/dimension entries; its heavier tolerance adds another 20 grams. [Frame](https://www.team-blacksheep.com/products/prod%3Asource1v6), [motor](https://shop.iflight.com/product-cat123/xing-x2806-5-fpv-nextgen-motor-pro1001), and propeller/battery links in the table.

For a screening estimate, allow **300–400 grams** more for the controller stack, Pi/camera/cooler, receiver, position/compass module, regulator, wiring, capacitor, printed mounts, straps and fasteners. This produces roughly **1.4–1.5 kilograms** ready to fly. That allowance is not a weighed assembly or a validated mount design; reserve further mass if later sensing is needed.

iFlight's matching nominal motor/propeller chart begins at **872 grams thrust per motor**, well above the roughly 350–375 grams per motor expected in hover here. It does not provide the required hover-power measurement. At maximum it records **42.33 amps per motor**, about 169 amps for four; the selected battery's continuous label corresponds to 112.5 amps. Thus neither a “55 amp” motor-controller label nor plenty of theoretical thrust establishes whole-system power suitability. [Manufacturer motor/propeller test chart](https://shop.iflight.com/image/catalog/TEST%20REPORT/XING-2806.5-1300KV.png).

The battery holds 99.9 watt-hours nominal. An illustrative 80% usable-energy allowance with a further 10 watt-hours kept for landing leaves about **420 watts average for ten useful minutes**, including all electronics and losses. This is a sensitivity calculation, not a selected reserve policy or flight-time prediction. Actual mass, battery sag, hover/mission power and recovery energy must be measured.
