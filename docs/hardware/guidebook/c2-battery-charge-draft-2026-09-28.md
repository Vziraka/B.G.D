The M450's 50 W ceiling means that even its 3 A setting would taper to at most about **2.98 A at 16.8 V** (50 ÷ 16.8; calculated, not a charging-time guarantee). The proposed first-charge setting here is **2.0 A**, about **0.32 C** for 6.2 Ah and below the charger's power limit throughout a standard 4S charge. This is a conservative engineering selection, **not a verified HOOVO-specific charge-rate instruction**. ## Release gates before the first powered connection

1. The owner has checked the actual pack and any included insert: **HOOVO, LiPo (not LiHV), 4S, 14.8 V nominal, 6200 mAh, EC5**. Record the exact printed charge-rate wording, if any. If the chemistry, count, connector or label differs or is unreadable, stop and revise this card.
2. Inspect the whole pack and both leads. There must be no swelling, cracked case, puncture, leaking, damaged insulation, loose balance wires, corroded/bent contacts, or warmth before charging. A defect means **no charge or aircraft use**. Let a warm pack return to room temperature before considering charging. Never charge the pack while mounted on the drone. [Spektrum LiPo safety](https://www.spektrumrc.com/ProdInfo/Files/LiPo_Charge_Manual_Low_Resolution.pdf).
3. Confirm an adult can supervise continuously, awake and in the room. Use a clear noncombustible surface away from combustibles and moisture; keep the charger's vents and AC plug accessible. Use the intact LiPo bag as an additional layer only if its actual instructions allow charging with leads exiting safely. Its listing does not establish fire containment. The M450 manual prohibits unattended use and heat/humidity/flammable surroundings. Charge only with charger ambient temperature within its [0–40 °C rating](https://www.toolkitrc.com/m450/).
4. With the main charge lead **disconnected from charger and pack**, verify its polarity with the meter in continuity/resistance mode. Black probe in `COM`, red in `V/Ω`, **not** the high-current socket. Confirm probe-touch reads connected and separated probes read open. Touch the XT60 contact marked `+` to the EC5 contact marked `+`: connected. Repeat `−` to `−`: connected. Each cross-pair (`+` to `−`) and the two contacts at either end: open. Trace red wire to `+`, black to `−` where visible. If signs, terminals or meter response are ambiguous, stop. **Never use continuity/ohms mode on the battery or powered charger.** The premade lead requires no soldering.
5. With the pack disconnected from everything, measure pack voltage **only if** the two recessed EC5 contacts are marked/traced and the insulated meter tips can contact one each without ever bridging them. Meter on DC volts, black `COM`, red `V/Ω`; red probe to pack `+`, black to `−`. A normal positive reading **12.8–16.8 V** is the draft screening range (3.2–4.2 V times four, conservative project criterion). A negative sign, `0`, below 12.8 V or above 16.8 V is a stop; it is not a prompt to change charger chemistry. If probing the recessed contacts is uncertain, **do not improvise with bare wire or poke adjacent balance pins**; keep this gate unresolved and use a qualified measurement method. A plausible total voltage does **not** prove each cell is healthy.
6. Confirm the pack has a five-wire keyed balance plug and that the selected five-wire extension reaches the M450's front balance port without force, offset, reversed orientation, bent pins or wire strain. Do not modify a plug or use a different cell-count slot to make it fit. The M450 manual's page 6 shows the **front left balance port** and **front right orange main port** from the display-facing view; rely on the actual port markings and keyed fit, not color alone.

**Unresolved before release:** the actual HOOVO label/insert and charge-rate wording; physical lead fit and polarity; pack voltage and condition; charge location/bag; whether this particular M450 shows cell voltages with only the balance lead attached. The manufacturer's manual documents individual cells on the **charging screen**, not a guaranteed pre-start review. Do not promise a pre-start screen gate or assume the charger has already rejected a bad cell.

## Proposed supervised connection and first check — use only after release

1. Leave the pack off the drone and place it on the prepared noncombustible surface, using the LiPo bag only as its actual instructions permit, with the charger **outside** it. Keep the charging setup clear of soldering and other bench work. With pack absent, connect the verified XT60 end of the premade main lead to the M450 main port and the five-wire extension to the M450 balance port. Leave their battery ends separated and protected from metal.
2. Plug in the M450 AC cord. After it boots, **short-press once to unlock**. **Long-press to select `4.20 V LiPo`** and **short-press to select `2.0 A`**. Read both selections aloud/on screen before any pack connection. If the actual pack instruction specifies a lower current, stop for a revised setting; never choose a higher-voltage chemistry to clear an error. The M450's cell count is inferred from the connected pack/lead; expect **four** displayed cells, not a separate `4S` menu. [M450 manual](https://download.toolkitrc.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/M450-Manual-V1.1-.pdf), pages 7–12.
3. Inspect the two charge connectors' molded `+`/`−`, keyed shapes and wire colors one last time. Mate the pack EC5 to the female EC5 on the verified lead **by the housings, straight and without force**. Keep the free balance plug away from the main wires/metal.
4. Mate the pack's five-wire balance plug to the already-installed five-wire extension **by the housings**. This is the final connection and can start charging **immediately**. Watch the charger continuously from the first second. If there is a spark beyond a tiny connector transient, melting, smoke, smell, heat or an error, disconnect AC if safely reachable and stop. Do not continue by reseating connectors live or switching chemistry.
5. Within the first **15 seconds**, verify screen: `Charging` (or a clear normal charging state), **4.20 V selected**, **2.0 A selected** (actual current can be lower), **four numbered cell voltages**, a positive 4S total within **12.8–16.8 V** initially, and each cell within **3.2–4.2 V**. Draft conservative imbalance stop: highest minus lowest cell **≤0.10 V** at the first reading. The 0.10 V criterion is a project screening threshold, **not an M450 or HOOVO specification**. Record the four numbers and total. If any number/count/condition differs, stop the process by unplugging AC (if safe), then isolate the battery when safe; report the exact display. Do not repeatedly restart.
6. Remain present and check the pack and screen periodically. Stop for charger error, decreasing cell count, any cell over **4.20 V**, new swelling, smell, smoke, damaged lead, or pack becoming hot to touch. Never leave the room or charge overnight. If a pack smokes or is actively heating, prioritize distance and local emergency response; do not handle the pack or reach across it. [M450 safety](https://download.toolkitrc.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/M450-Manual-V1.1-.pdf), page 3; [Spektrum general LiPo safety](https://www.spektrumrc.com/ProdInfo/Files/LiPo_Charge_Manual_Low_Resolution.pdf).
7. On an **unambiguous charger-complete indication**, record the exact status, total voltage, four cell voltages and elapsed time. The published M450 manual does **not** document the precise completion text, so a slowing current or a generic `F` power-limit icon alone is not proof of completion. If the finish state is unclear, stop supervision-free continuation: turn off AC and report the screen for review. Normal finished-cell ceiling is **4.20 V each / 16.8 V total**, not a requirement to force every display digit to exactly 4.200. Any cell over that ceiling fails.
8. With normal charge finished, disconnect the pack's **balance plug from the extension**, then its **EC5 from the main lead**, holding connector housings rather than pulling wires. Unplug charger AC and keep the isolated pack in the approved safe location. Do not leave it connected to charger or aircraft. If stopping for an abnormal condition while current is flowing, disconnect AC first **only if safely reachable**; disconnect battery leads after the situation is safe.

## Storage instead of a full charge

If no near-term powered test is ready, leave a healthy pack near storage state rather than charging it to full just to wait. The [M450 product page](https://www.toolkitrc.com/m450/) specifies a **3.85 V/cell LiPo storage mode** that can charge or discharge to that target. To use it, the pack must first be disconnected; with the charger powered and no pack attached, unlock, select **`3.85 V LiPoStorage`** and the approved current, verify the selection, then repeat the same supervised connection and four-cell checks. The M450's discharge power/time is not documented here; do not promise a quick full-to-storage cycle. Disconnect and record the four cells when the charger clearly finishes. Never use `3.85 V` as the full-flight charge mode.
