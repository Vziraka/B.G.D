ON THE OTHER COMPUTER
1. Extract this ZIP into a folder. Open pi-carrier-x1e-petg.3mf in Bambu
   Studio as a PROJECT, retaining its saved settings (not geometry only).
   Using Bambu Studio 02.08.02.61 matches the verified version.
2. Check printer Bambu Lab X1E, standard 0.4 mm hardened-steel nozzle,
   Textured PEI Plate, and the saved BGD Polymaker PETG provisional profile.
   The owner confirms gold Textured PEI and PETG physically in AMS slot 4.
   Printer login/connection and physical AMS assignments are local setup;
   transferring this file does not configure them.
3. Slice plate and inspect Preview. Expected size is 57 x 78 x 10.2 mm,
   with feet down and one carrier. Prior verified estimate: 1h 2m 52s and
   13.17 g including supports/brim. Versions/calibration may alter estimates.
4. Before sending, map PROJECT FILAMENT 1 to PHYSICAL AMS SLOT 4 (PETG).
   The project filament number is not the physical AMS slot number.
   Verify the spool's actual material and the correct physical X1E.
   STOP BEFORE SEND/PRINT. The owner explicitly requires manual approval.
   Filament loading and this file transfer are not approval to print.

EXPECTED SAVED SETTINGS
PETG nozzle 250 C / textured bed 65 C; chamber target 0; auxiliary fan 0.
0.20 mm layers, 4 walls, 5 top/bottom layers, 30% gyroid infill.
Outer walls 40 mm/s, inner walls 60 mm/s, first layer 20 mm/s;
maximum material flow 8 mm3/s; cooling 20-60%.
Normal/grid supports FROM BUILD PLATE ONLY, top gap 0.2 mm, XY gap 0.4 mm.
Outer brim 3 mm, brim separation 0.2 mm. Do not rotate or scale the model.
The standard X1E start sequence includes a brief hotter purge; the saved
250 C setting is the part-printing temperature.

VERIFICATION AND LIMITS
Model vertices match source to 0.001 mm. Bambu slice had no warnings.
Generated supports do not enter the four tie tunnels. critical-layers.png
shows five checked heights; review.json records digital checks.
Actual adhesion, support removal, tie/standoff fit and strength are not
physically tested. After an approved first print, cool and remove supports,
then check flat rails, clear tie tunnels and standoff seating without force.
This is a fit prototype, not a flight-qualified part.
