BGD - ALL SEVEN PIECES ON ONE PLATE
Verified September 28, 2026 with Bambu Studio 02.08.02.61.

CONTENTS
1 Pi carrier, 1 camera bracket, 1 forward-depth bracket, 2 battery end
stops, 1 electronics-stack adapter, 1 electronics-flow support.
Six models produce seven separate physical pieces in ONE print job.
Do not print additional copies of the paired battery stops.

PRINTER / MATERIAL
Bambu Lab X1E, standard 0.4 mm hardened-steel nozzle, gold Textured PEI
Plate, 1.75 mm Polymaker PETG. Saved settings: 250 C nozzle, 65 C bed,
0.20 mm layers, 4 walls, 5 top/bottom layers, 30% gyroid, outer brim 3 mm.
Chamber target 0; auxiliary fan off; part fan 20-60%; max flow 8 mm3/s.
The standard X1E startup can use a hotter purge than the print temperature.

HOW TO USE
1. Extract this ZIP and open BGD-All-Parts-X1E-PETG.3mf in Bambu Studio
   AS A PROJECT, keeping its settings. Do not import geometry only.
2. Keep saved positions, orientation, scale, and per-object settings.
   Global support is OFF; overrides correctly enable it for THREE parts.
   Do not switch all support on/off or remove the six support blockers.
   Print sequence is BY LAYER, so all pieces build together.
3. Slice plate. Confirm X1E / 0.4 / Textured PEI / PETG and inspect preview.
   Expected estimate is approximately 3 hours / 39.62 g including supports
   and brims. Timing can vary with slicer version and printer calibration.
4. Map project filament 1 to the actual PETG slot. On the owner's machine
   PETG was reported in AMS slot 4; confirm it is still PETG. On another
   machine use its actual PETG slot. Project filament 1 is NOT AMS slot 1.
5. Obtain the owner's explicit manual approval BEFORE sending or starting.
   This package does not constitute that approval. No job has been sent.
6. Let parts cool, remove brims and temporary supports, and inspect.
   Pi: remove support under the elevated ring, keeping four tie tunnels open.
   Camera: remove tree branches/feet, including those in base tie slots;
   protect the small screw bosses. Depth bracket: remove boss supports.
   Battery stops, stack adapter and flow support have supports disabled.
   Deliver all seven pieces; physical fit/strength checks still follow.
