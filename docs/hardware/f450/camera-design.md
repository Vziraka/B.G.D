## Documented interfaces

The [official standard Camera Module 3 drawing](https://datasheets.raspberrypi.com/camera/camera-module-3-standard-mechanical-drawing.pdf), visually inspected September 22, gives a 25 × 23.862 mm board. Mount holes are Ø2.2 mm, 2 mm from each side, and at 2 and 14.5 mm above the board bottom: **21 × 12.5 mm hole pattern**. The mount holds the camera PCB bottom at local Z14 and rear plane at Y−3, with 3 mm projecting bosses and an open central rear area. Four Ø4 mm bosses touch only the small mounting-hole regions; no lens housing, autofocus assembly or ribbon latch is clamped. The drawing's Ø4.75 mounting area and 2 mm edge offset constrain screw-head/washer diameter; use compact heads ≤4.5 mm and avoid oversized washers. The model holes are Ø2.3 mm print-clearance trials; do not alter the camera's holes.

The [Pololu 3418 product page](https://www.pololu.com/product/3418) expressly specifies two M2-compatible holes 0.5 inch apart. Its [manufacturer dimension drawing](https://www.pololu.com/file/0J1995/vl53l7cx-carrier-dimension-diagram.pdf) gives the precise board layout and hole diameter Ø2.18 mm, drill tolerance ±0.1 mm and board-edge tolerance ±0.3 mm. The bracket rotates the board into landscape: 17.8 mm runs horizontally, 12.7 mm vertically, with holes **12.7 mm apart** along its bottom region. PCB bottom is local Z15.5, rear face Y−3, mounting row Z18; the 2.5 mm hole-to-edge callout is rounded metric, while the explicitly stated 0.5 inch pitch is used for hole spacing. Two Ø4 mm bosses bear only around those holes. The photographed board rear is substantially plain, but the open support keeps the central rear clear regardless. Header/wire edge is uppermost; its 2.54 mm pins and soldered wires need space above the board.

## Hardware and anchoring

**Additional small hardware is needed:** six M2 machine screws, six M2 nuts, and only compatible compact bearing/locking hardware as needed. M2×10 is the draft starting length: about 1.0–1.12 mm PCB + 3 mm boss + 2.5 mm rail + roughly 1.6 mm nut leaves about 1.8 mm nominal thread excess. Verify chosen screw head, actual nut thickness and engagement rather than treating this estimate as a purchase or established fit. Rear nuts remain accessible above the low buttresses. No M2 hardware is in the current confirmed buying snapshot; the purchased M2.5 screws must not be forced into these boards. No order was placed.

Use two ordered nominal 150 × 3 mm zip ties per bracket. Each base has two **4 mm-wide elongated slots**: camera slots center X±8, end-center Y15..27; depth X±6, end-center Y14..23. Route around a solid lower-plate strip through existing openings, capturing both bracket and plate with separated ties. Rounded slot ends permit fore/aft positioning. The exact plate outline is still approximate; secure broad bearing and a non-sliding route matter more than numerical hole coincidence. Do not repurpose motor/arm/landing screws. Keep ties, tie heads and fasteners clear of power joints/capacitor/wires; no printed base should rest on exposed solder pads.

## Optical and cable clearances

Camera drawing specifies **66° horizontal / 41° vertical** field of view; the depth sensor has **60° × 60°** typical square FOV ([Pololu](https://www.pololu.com/product/3418)). All structural rails and bosses are **behind the PCBs**, so there is no printed front bezel or hood to clip either optical cone. Camera lens center is approximately local Z28.4 from the drawing; in the proposed landscape depth orientation its optical center is approximately local Z23.1. Those heights are planning values, not boresight calibration.

The two proposed sensing boards are side by side with unobstructed space immediately ahead. Check the complete assembly's arms, battery wiring and future lights against both cones. Neither the bracket mesh nor an isolated CAD render proves a clear live image or correct depth readings. Keep lights and shiny edges out of the sensor's immediate optical path; do not add an uncalibrated clear cover over the depth sensor.

The owned camera ribbon is 200 mm. Reserve at least approximately 50 mm for broad bends, connector approaches and strain relief; aim for a routed centerline path no longer than approximately 150 mm in the proposed lower-front-camera/top-center-Pi arrangement. This is a planning allowance, not a specified minimum bend radius. The open rear between the camera's two rails admits the ribbon; no channel fixes its width or forces a sharp fold. Anchor the ribbon gently at a separate nearby retained cable point, with slack at both latches, and keep it away from propeller sweep, sharp frame edges, fan and hot/current-carrying components. The lower camera placement requires checking the route past the top-plate edge, not just straight-line endpoint distance.

## Verification and remaining physical check

Both models compiled with portable **OpenSCAD 2021.01** to STL without warnings, with CGAL reporting simple geometry. STL edge incidence checked: every mesh edge is shared by two triangles. Signed tetrahedral volume gives 2995.68 and 2188.64 mm³; the table uses 1.3 g/cm³ PETG and excludes hardware/ties/boards. Renders were visually inspected for open apertures, accessible holes and base slots. These checks establish digital geometry only; slicer, print orientation/support, layer strength, long-term creep and flight vibration are not qualified. In particular horizontal screw bores/bosses require suitable print orientation/support rather than an assumed support-free print.

No further photo or dimension survey is needed to advance this design. At the normal disconnected fit session, check the actual M2 screws/boss contact, no component pressure, ribbon/header access, broad base bearing and resistance to rocking/sliding. Then inspect live camera corners and depth readings with the full frame present. These final interface observations cannot be replaced by a generic board drawing; they need not block CAD work now.
