## Product direction

- Natural phone voice supports following, person/object search and scouting/counting, including multi-step requests. Orbit/watch/patrol are optional extensions. Cargo and recipient-release features are retired.
- Launch by paired phone/radio remains distinct from onboard face/liveness permission for new spoken commands. Every person enrolled through the unlocked paired phone has the same command abilities; enrollment/removal is landed, disarmed and motor-inhibited. Independent manual takeover and controlled recovery remain available.
- Live camera video and digital zoom are required; the aircraft may reposition for useful detail where conditions permit. Actual sensing must support the desired outdoor, large-warehouse, dark and out-of-sight missions before operation is qualified.
- Essential operation is offline, with optional online language assistance. Internet loss and command-link loss are separate events. The initial 100 m target is distance from the user, not total travel or a qualified link range.
- Endurance means 10 useful minutes plus takeoff and landing; battery monitoring interrupts work with enough energy to reach a suitable landing spot. Whole-mission completion prediction is not required.
- Preserve the existing iPhone direction for assessment. [Remote Mac research](../research/iphone-route-recheck-2026-09-10.md) remains the native-build starting point, with no provider selected or purchased. EarPods are optional phone-input equipment. The Windows PC remains a development host; the standalone PC biometric product is dormant.
