# Pi natural conversation acceptance — 26 September 2026

The owner wants natural conversation about drone and camera tasks, including corrections and questions about current state. This does not grant the interpreter authority over flight, device setup, files, purchases or security controls. The existing interaction contract remains the product requirement: a proposed mission is reviewed, authenticated execution checks capability and conditions, and essential operation remains offline.

## Gates

1. **Meaning.** A qualifying trial has zero consequential failures among the 49 critical cases and at least 95% correct among the 51 ordinary cases (at least 49/51). Report the exact-machine score and a separate human review of apparent synonyms. Count a wording variant as equivalent only if the same executor would choose the same action and grounded target. Never waive a dropped clause, reversed negation, invented reference or bypass request as harmless. Report cold, median and p95 response time and all failures, including timeouts.
2. **System boundary.** Regressions must show that model output cannot execute directly; Stop bypasses model delay; unsupported/multi-step requests do not become partial executable missions; unknown targets cause clarification; corrections retire older proposals; confirmation binds to one exact proposal and is consumed once. Actual scene grounding and a truthful status answer need their own integration checks. Passing the text corpus alone does not pass this gate.
3. **Pi coexistence.** Only after meaning and boundary gates, test the candidate on the owned Pi with active camera/target tracking and speech recognition. Record cold/warm proposal time, RAM, CPU, temperature, throttle flags, frame rates and displayed delay. Preserve the existing minimum 15 fps vision/display and maximum 300 ms delay, under 80 C, zero power/throttle flags and no service errors through a ten-minute active workload. The prior static-view run cleared the display floor by only 0.050 fps; it is not an active-language pass. A provisional user-facing target is five seconds median and ten seconds p95 from end of speech to reviewed proposal; measure before treating that target as met.
4. **Real conversation.** After automated work, use one owner-attended physical session for spoken paraphrases, pause-separated corrections, no/stop, ambiguous references, unsupported mixtures and proposal confirmation while the camera is tracking. Score speech transcription separately from interpretation. Do not repeat unchanged previously accepted camera tests. The browser desk remains simulation-only; native phone identity, pairing and airborne missions have separate later gates.

## If a gate fails

Classify the failure before changing anything: interface normalization, model semantics, actual visual grounding, speech transcription or Pi resource contention. Correct a true interface defect once and rerun the development cases. For persistent semantic errors, compare a distinct local model family, then consider a conservative interpretation design that asks for clarification when clauses cannot be accounted for. Do not tune against this held-out set; create a new sealed set after any design change informed by its results. For Pi overload, try a smaller validated quantization or workload scheduling, then evaluate phone-local or additional onboard compute against power, mass, thermal, cost and useful-flight-time requirements. Optional online command-text assistance can add coverage but cannot replace the offline essentials. Any unmet product requirement remains open and is reported with a measured blocker and concrete next option.

GPT-6 Sol at max effort owns consequential decisions and final review. One GPT-6 Luna at max effort worker owns bounded implementation/testing. Do not add agents for routine subtasks. No new hardware, paid service, biometric upload or aircraft actuation is authorized by this acceptance record.

The desk may explain that it cannot ground that person yet. Do not present a parsed plan as an observation or a completed flight action.
