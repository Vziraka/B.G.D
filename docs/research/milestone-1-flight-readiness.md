## What the primary guidance establishes

- **First-flight commissioning is not ordinary practice.** ArduPilot warns that an untuned multirotor's first takeoff can involve unexpected power increases, instability or insufficient control. It recommends prior simulator or training-aircraft practice, correct radio calibration, motor/frame verification and careful evaluation of initial flight behavior. Its first objective is a stable aircraft suitable for further testing, not immediate use of every supported capability. [Initial Tuning Flight](https://ardupilot.org/copter/docs/initial-tuning-flight.html)
- **Assembly and configuration require their own review.** ArduPilot separates mounting the controller, connecting receiver/power/motors, and initial configuration/calibration from first-flight tuning. This supports checking the actual completed installation, not treating an approved shopping list or successful simulator flying as proof of aircraft readiness. The latter distinction is our inference. [First Time Setup](https://ardupilot.org/copter/docs/initial-setup.html)
- **Pre-arm failures must be resolved.** Automated checks detect issues including incomplete calibration, configuration errors and unhealthy sensor data. ArduPilot explicitly warns against bypassing failed checks to fly. Passing these checks is not stated to certify every mechanical or operational hazard. [Pre-Arm Safety Checks](https://ardupilot.org/copter/docs/common-prearm-safety-checks.html)
- **Ground checks need propulsion-safe conditions.** The motor documentation calls for propeller removal during motor-direction and motor-order testing. These are separate from airborne tuning; this report does not reproduce a powered test sequence. [Connect ESCs and Motors](https://ardupilot.org/copter/docs/connect-escs-and-motors.html)
- **Failsafe configuration needs verification.** The preflight checklist includes radio, battery and navigation-related failsafes. The radio-failsafe documentation describes ground testing, including tests without a propulsion battery and propeller removal if one is connected. Appropriate responses still depend on the actual configuration. No deliberate airborne link loss, battery exhaustion, threshold or recovery mode is selected here. [Pre-Flight Checklist](https://ardupilot.org/copter/docs/checklist.html), [Radio Failsafe](https://ardupilot.org/copter/docs/radio-failsafe.html)
