## Proposed PC acceptance contract

### Usability measurement

1. Separate development/enrollment material from final evaluation. Freeze application/model versions, profile, prompts, thresholds, supported capture paths, ordinary-use conditions and the outcome rules before the evaluation. Document light/noise conditions without inventing instrument readings. Do not silently adapt the profile or tune on the evaluation trials.
2. Use the approved five-day/100-check schedule. Each counted trial is the first attempt for one fresh harmless desk action. It is not a retry, repeated frame or reenrollment. Distribute the agreed normal-use conditions in advance and include the first check after reopening in each session.
3. **Reliability proposal:** at least **95/100** first attempts must authorize. An eventual retry does not replace a failed first attempt. An in-scope cancellation, no-input failure, device fault or timeout during a started trial remains visible and does not become a hidden retry. Predeclare how an external interruption can make a trial not assessable; show every such event and its replacement separately instead of selectively deleting adverse outcomes.
4. **Timing proposal:** every successful baseline trial must finish within **ten seconds** from starting the request to showing authorization, including prompts. A success at eleven seconds is a reliability success and a timing failure. A fast rejection is not a success. Report counts plus successful-duration median, 95th percentile and maximum, and separately report rejection/timeout/retry durations. The sixty-second expiry never converts a slow success into a timing pass.
5. Time the complete normal first-enrollment workflow separately against its five-minute target. Record failed/restarted setup attempts, required assistance and troubleshooting separately; do not hide them by reporting only the final successful segment. The target begins after the app and required models are installed.
6. After a fix, preserve the failing case as regression evidence and use fresh held-out sessions/material for confirmation of the revised version. Label exploratory, regression and final evaluation results distinctly.

This adopts a transparent observed first evaluation, not a new statistical guarantee. In particular, **95/100 does not establish a true future success probability of at least 95%**, and splitting checks over days does not make all observations independent. Report the observed result and limitations; do not add an unapproved confidence-bound requirement that could make the agreed 100-trial schedule incapable of passing. These interpretations are consolidated from the [PC acceptance proposal](m1-pc-acceptance-proposal-after-q59.md); no new experiment or statistical calculation was performed here.

### Security coverage and stopping rule

The existing 90-attempt outline is an **exploratory starting allocation**, not a security acceptance quota. It comprises thirty live nonowner attempts and twelve attempts in each of five spoof/injection families. Repeating weak attacks ninety times, or rejecting everyone because capture is broken, does not establish that the gate resists the required attacks. The final reviewed matrix must be tailored to the implemented challenges and supported capture paths. [Existing coverage proposal](m1-pc-acceptance-proposal-after-q59.md)

| Required coverage | Meaningful evidence for the final matrix |
| --- | --- |
| Live nonowners | Three individually consenting adults; fresh full-gate attempts across sessions. Report the small convenience sample without claiming population false-match performance. |
| Physical face attacks | Owner-targeted printed/screen photographs and video, varied source material and presentation conditions; challenge-aware cases where relevant. |
| Physical audio attacks | Owner-targeted recorded voice playback and consented generated/converted voice, including relevant challenge wording and more than one meaningful method where obtainable. |
| Digital capture substitution | The actual supported camera and microphone input routes: camera-only, microphone-only and paired substitution. Model-only file tests cannot stand in for the application's capture route. |
| Channel isolation | For each spoof family, attack one channel while the consenting owner supplies the other genuine channel, plus paired attacks where applicable. Record component and full-gate decisions so one rejected channel does not hide failure of the other. |
| Working controls | Legitimate positive controls before/after the relevant batches, confirmation that attack input reaches the intended path, and explicit attacker-access assumptions. A broken path produces incomplete evidence, not a security pass. |
| State and alternate paths | First setup, ordinary update, lost-code replacement, lockout recovery, missing/tampered state, account switching, one-action authorization, duplicate/concurrent requests, cooldowns, deadlines and interrupted operations. |

Before final evaluation, review whether each threat-model requirement has a realistic case, working input/control, expected result and retained evidence. Any unavailable tool/method or unsupported path is an explicit coverage gap. Do not silently replace generated-voice testing with a generic synthetic stranger voice, or software substitution with a deliberately broken verifier. The report does not claim all conceivable attacks can be exhausted.

**Stopping rule:** one unauthorized action approval blocks security signoff until investigated, fixed and retested. A successful channel spoof blocked by the other channel remains a recorded weakness to assess against the required design; it cannot be claimed as that channel detecting the attack. Missing mandatory coverage or unresolved storage/recovery failures means **incomplete**, even with zero unauthorized approvals. No pooled “0 out of 90” universal attack-success claim is permitted. These are proposed operationalizations of the existing security requirements and research, not new proven defenses. [PC proposal](m1-pc-acceptance-proposal-after-q59.md), [retry/recovery proposal](pc-retry-timeout-recovery-options.md)

### Bounded test-data plan for later consent

The following is a **recommended planning policy**, not participant consent or authorization to capture. Present the concrete session, tooling, assets, deletion deadline and consequences to each affected person before collecting or generating anything. Do not contact participants or ask for names now.

- **Normal app behavior stays unchanged:** discard raw ordinary enrollment/check captures after processing; retain only the encrypted enrolled references needed by the app. Do not add a general debug recording mode to obtain attack data incidentally.
- **Security assets only:** separately consented owner images, audio, clips and owner-targeted generated/converted media stay on the existing local PC in a restricted encrypted test location. No cloud upload, online cloning service, shared repository, remote telemetry or third-party transfer is part of this proposal. If a needed method cannot operate within that boundary, show the gap and obtain a separate reviewed decision rather than uploading data automatically.
- **Bounded lifetime proposal:** delete each raw or derived attack asset on completion of its evaluation/retest use, or **thirty days after creation, whichever comes first**. A retest needing material beyond that deadline requires a new explicit retention decision or newly consented capture; no automatic extension. The five-day owner schedule can fit this window, but delays may require new material.
- **Nonowner default:** process live nonowner input transiently with no retained raw recordings or enrolled nonowner profile. Any recording needed for a specific diagnostic case requires that participant's separate explicit consent. Participation does not enroll a guest or confer permission to command the aircraft.
- **Result records:** retain only trial IDs, coarse condition labels, durations, configuration hashes, outcomes and non-biometric failure notes. Do not log recovery codes, biometric templates, raw media, real names or identifying file paths. Keep detailed pseudonymous trial records locally for at most **ninety days after the evaluation is completed or abandoned**; retain only the non-identifying aggregate evaluation report/configuration provenance in project documentation. These periods are proposals chosen to bound retention, not legal requirements.
- **Withdrawal and cleanup:** stop further use when a participant withdraws; delete their retained assets and identifiable linkage promptly, record the resulting coverage gap and arrange a newly consented replacement evaluation if needed. Explain exactly what aggregate results remain. Verify application-managed files/logs are removed; do not promise forensic erasure from every memory, pagefile, SSD or external backup copy.

Specific local privacy and consent obligations must be checked before an actual session without requesting personal/location details in this planning task. The preceding policy derives from the existing local-only/explicit-permission recommendation in the [PC proposal](m1-pc-acceptance-proposal-after-q59.md); the thirty- and ninety-day bounds are new proposals and are not already approved.

| Remaining detail | Requirement the design must preserve | Spec/evidence obligation |

Distinguish unavailable input from an evaluated rejection without a bypass. Freeze this before usability/security evaluation. |
| Concurrent requests and alternative entry points | Every new protected action needs its own full check; five-failure throttling cannot be bypassed | Define a single authoritative attempt policy across windows/processes and deterministic handling of duplicate or simultaneous requests; no hidden queues of unthrottled evaluations. |
| Restart, sleep, clocks and damaged control state | App reopening does not clear an active wait or failure streak; fixed thirty-second continuing-failure policy; no new permanent-ban rule | Specify durable state and elapsed-time handling, late callbacks and validation of damaged state. | Completed approval freshness | One requested action only; no reusable unlocked session or replay after restart | Prefer immediate execution of the harmless bound desk action after a final state check, avoiding a stored reusable approval. If an asynchronous boundary requires a token, specify request binding, single use, bounded lifetime and final execution checks. Do not reuse the sixty-second unfinished-request limit as its lifetime without review. |
| Recovery-code checking | Code plus fresh designated-owner Windows verification; neither alone authorizes recovery; recovery grants no action approval | Select code generation/storage and guessing controls, including concurrency, interrupted checks and offline Windows-account binding. | Atomic recovery completion | Old committed profile/code remain before successful replacement; new complete profile/code become authoritative together after success; new code saved offline before completion | Define a durable transaction/restart protocol and interruption tests at every transition. | Ordinary profile update | Existing face, voice and fresh owner Windows verification; preserve last complete profile until replacement succeeds; no silent learning | Specify validation and durable replacement separately from recovery. Test failure, interruption and account switching without granting action approval. |
| Capture and confidentiality | Required spoof/substitution coverage; encrypted references; raw ordinary captures not retained | Select accessible hardware/software paths and define allowed trust assumptions. Device names or an IR-camera listing are not proof of liveness or a trusted path. Inspect temporary files, logs and crash handling. |

## Aircraft acceptance contract and implementation boundary

Keep the previously proposed separate gates: simulator preparation, actual completed-build review, commissioning, repeatability demonstration and final closure. Simulator competence cannot establish that a solder joint, motor arrangement or actual configuration is correct. Approved three-run/two-session repetition remains one part of M1, not a substitute for the other gates. [Flight acceptance proposal](m1-flight-acceptance-proposal-after-q59.md)

Before any real acceptance attempt, the selected aircraft/firmware/configuration and privately assessed site must support a reviewed commissioning and basic-flight protocol. That protocol must set the operating mode, hover period, position/height bounds, movement/landing area, weather constraints, energy/reserve rules, observation method, stopping rules and evidence handling. **This review adopts no universal numerical flight tolerances.** Freeze suitable values before trials, and do not retrospectively widen them to award a pass.

Record each attempt as pass, skill criterion missed, safety stop or not assessable. An interrupted run is not a pass; a skill miss breaks the consecutive-pass sequence. Any safety problem invokes the existing grounding rule until investigated, resolved and the affected checks pass. The final protocol must settle how a prudent weather/conditions stop affects a continuing sequence; safety must never be subordinated to completing the count. Do not deliberately induce airborne link loss, low-energy emergencies or motor shutdown for this evaluation.

The owner remains a novice working solo; no helper requirement is newly approved. AI is not physical inspection or flight supervision. If readiness cannot be established, aircraft progression stops while independent safe PC/simulator work can continue. The user privately verifies applicable aviation, site, radio, privacy and other requirements before operation. This report is not an executable flight checklist or permission to fly.

The later full-equipment twenty-useful-minute mission plus separate reserve remains a planning/performance requirement. It is not silently replaced by a short M1 manual-flight exercise, and detailed future mission validation is not required to have already run before writing M1 software. Hardware feasibility must nevertheless support an honest path to that requirement and the whole-project budget.
